# Department Management

Departments are the fundamental building blocks of an Organization Chart.

Each department defines:

- Organizational hierarchy
- Department members
- Department representatives
- Reporting relationships

Departments can be created, updated, reorganized, or removed while maintaining the integrity of the organization structure.

---

## Department Structure

Each department contains three types of information.

| Category                  | Description                              |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Department Information    | Department name and code.                |
| Employees                 | Members assigned to the department.      |
| Organization Relationship | Parent department and child departments. |

Every department belongs to exactly one Organization Chart.

---

## Create a Department

Departments can be created from any existing department.

Open the department menu and select **Create Department**.

![Create Department](/images/create-department.png)

Administrators can configure:

| Field             | Required | Description                                      |
| ----------------- | :------: | ------------------------------------------------ |
| Parent Department |    ✅    | Department that will contain the new department. |
| Department Code   | Optional | Internal department identifier.                  |
| Department Name   |    ✅    | Display name of the department.                  |

After saving, the department is immediately added beneath its parent.

---

## Parent Department

Every department, except the root department, belongs to a parent department.

Example:

```text
Company
   │
   ├── Sales
   │
   └── Engineering
           │
           ├── Frontend
           └── Backend
```

Changing the parent department automatically changes the hierarchy.

---

### Child Department

![Department Menu](/images/department-menu.png)

Available actions:

---

## Update Department

Administrators can update department information at any time.

Editable information includes:

- Parent department
- Department code
- Department name

Changing the parent department immediately updates the organization hierarchy.

---

## Delete Department

Workflow supports two deletion methods.

### Delete Department

Removes only the selected department.

Employees and child departments must first be reassigned or removed.

---

### Delete Department Branch

Removes the selected department together with every descendant department.

```text
Engineering
    │
    ├── Frontend
    ├── Backend
    └── QA
```

Deleting the branch removes the entire subtree.

<Callout type="warning">
  Delete Department Branch permanently removes every child department within the
  selected branch.
</Callout>

# Employee Management within Organization Chart

Employees can be managed directly without leaving the Organization Chart.

This allows administrators to complete organizational changes in one workspace.

---

## Add Existing Employee

Select **Add Employee** from the department menu.

![Add Employee](/images/add-employee-department.png)

Configure:

| Field      | Description       |
| ---------- | ----------------- |
| Department | Target department |
| Employee   | Existing employee |
| Role       | Department role   |

After saving, the employee becomes a member of the department.

---

## Register a New Employee

If the employee does not already exist, select **Create new employee**.

Workflow opens the employee registration dialog.

![Register Employee](/images/register-new-employee.png)

Administrators can configure:

| Field              | Required |
| ------------------ | :------: |
| Full Name          |    ✅    |
| Email              |    ✅    |
| Department         | Optional |
| Code               | Optional |
| Role               | Optional |
| Approval Authority | Optional |

After registration, the employee is automatically assigned to the selected department.

---

## Department Roles

Each employee can have a role within a department.

Examples include:

- Engineer
- Team Leader
- Manager
- General Manager

Department Roles describe an employee's organizational responsibility.

They are independent from:

- Application approval levels
- Workflow permissions
- System administrator permissions

---

# Approval Authority

Approval Authority identifies employees who officially represent a department.

Employees with Approval Authority receive two special behaviors:

- They are displayed directly on the Organization Chart.
- They can manage department-level applications.

![Employee Details](/images/employee-details.png)

---

## Display Behavior

Workflow intentionally limits the number of employees displayed on each department node.

Only employees marked with **Approval Authority** are displayed.

Example:

```text
Sales Department

Alice
Department Manager

Bob
General Manager

+18 employee(s)

See all
```

Remaining employees are available through the department's employee list.

This design keeps large Organization Charts readable.

---

## Employee Details

Department assignments can be updated at any time.

Editable information includes:

- Department
- Department Role
- Approval Authority

Employee name and email cannot be modified from the Organization Chart.

Those properties are managed from **Employee Management**.

## Business Rules

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Can a department exist without employees?">

Yes.

Departments may exist before employees are assigned.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can employees be added directly from the Organization Chart?">

Yes.

Administrators may either assign an existing employee or register a new employee without leaving the Organization Chart.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can more than one employee have Approval Authority?">

Yes.

A department may contain multiple employees with Approval Authority.

All of them are displayed directly on the Organization Chart.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between Department Role and Approval Authority?">

Department Role describes the employee's position within the department.

Approval Authority identifies department representatives and grants permission to manage department-level applications.

The two properties are independent.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can Employee Name or Email be edited here?">

No.

Only department-related information can be updated from the Organization Chart.

Personal information must be managed from the Employee Management module.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>
