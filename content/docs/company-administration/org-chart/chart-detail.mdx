# Organization Chart Details

After selecting an Organization Chart from the list, administrators are taken to the **Chart Details** page.

This workspace is where the organizational structure is designed and maintained.

From this page, administrators can:

- Build the department hierarchy.
- Assign employees to departments.
- Configure department representatives.
- Search employees within departments.
- View the complete organization structure.
- Switch between hierarchy and list views.

![Organization Chart Details](/images/org-chart-detail.png)

---

## Page Layout

The Chart Details page consists of three main areas.

| Area                   | Description                                                |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Header                 | Displays the chart name, applied date, and current status. |
| Organization Workspace | Displays the department hierarchy.                         |
| Toolbar                | Provides display mode switching and refresh actions.       |

---

## Organization Workspace

The center workspace displays the organization hierarchy as a tree.

Each department is represented as a node connected to its parent department.

```text
Company
    │
    ├── Sales
    │      ├── Domestic
    │      └── International
    │
    ├── Human Resources
    │
    └── Engineering
           ├── Frontend
           └── Backend
```

The hierarchy represents the reporting relationship between departments.

---

## Department Hierarchy

Departments are organized using a parent-child relationship.

Each department can contain:

- Child departments
- Employees
- Department representatives

The hierarchy can have multiple levels depending on the company's organizational structure.

<Callout type="info">
  The root department represents the company itself and acts as the top-level
  department in the organization.
</Callout>

---

## Moving Departments

Departments can be reorganized using drag-and-drop.

Administrators can:

- Drag a department.
- Drop it onto another department.
- Automatically update the hierarchy.

![Department Hierarchy](/images/org-chart-detail.png)

<Callout type="tip">
  Moving a department also moves all of its child departments and assigned
  employees.
</Callout>

---

## Department Node

Each department is displayed as a card containing key information.

A department node includes:

- Department name
- Employee search
- Department representatives
- Department menu
- Employee summary

![Department Node](/images/department-node.png)

---

### Employee Search

Each department provides a search box for quickly locating employees within that department.

Searching only affects the selected department and does not search the entire organization.

---

### Department Representatives

Only employees with **Approval Authority** are displayed directly on the department node.

These employees represent the department and are immediately visible within the organization chart.

Other department members remain hidden inside the department's employee list.

<Callout type="warning">
  Approval Authority does not determine approval levels. It identifies employees
  who represent the department and are displayed directly on the Organization
  Chart.
</Callout>

---

### Additional Employees

When a department contains more employees than can be displayed, Workflow summarizes the remaining employees.

Example:

```text
+7 employee(s)

See all
```

Selecting **See all** opens the complete employee list for that department.

---

## Employee List

Click **Employee List** to view every employee assigned to the current Organization Chart.

![Employee List](/images/org-chart-employee-list.png)

The Employee List allows administrators to:

- Search employees.
- View department assignments.
- View employee roles.
- Assign employees to departments.

---

## Assign Employees

Employees can be assigned to departments directly from the Employee List.

Click the **+** button beside an employee.

Workflow opens the assignment dialog.

![Assign Employee](/images/setup-department-role.png)

Administrators can configure:

| Field      | Description                          |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Employee   | Employee being assigned.             |
| Department | Target department.                   |
| Role       | Employee role within the department. |

After saving, the employee becomes part of the selected department.

---

## Multiple Department Assignments

An employee may belong to more than one department.

Each assignment is managed independently.

This allows organizations to support:

- Matrix organizations.
- Cross-functional teams.
- Temporary project assignments.

---

## Display Modes

The Organization Chart supports two viewing modes.

<Tabs items={["Hierarchy", "List"]}>

<Tab>

Displays the complete organization as a hierarchical tree.

Recommended when managing department relationships.

</Tab>

<Tab>

Displays department information in a structured list.

Useful for reviewing large organizational structures.

</Tab>

## Business Rules

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Can a department contain child departments?">

Yes.

Departments can contain any number of child departments.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can employees belong to multiple departments?">

Yes.

Each department assignment is managed independently.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who appears on the department node?">

Only employees with **Approval Authority** are displayed directly on the Organization Chart.

Other employees remain available in the department's employee list.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does moving a department affect its children?">

Yes.

Moving a department automatically moves all descendant departments and assigned employees.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>
