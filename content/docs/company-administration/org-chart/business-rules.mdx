# Organization Chart Business Rules

This section summarizes the key business rules governing Organization Charts, departments, and employee assignments.

Understanding these rules helps ensure that organizational changes are applied consistently throughout Workflow.

---

## Organization Chart Rules

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Only one Organization Chart can be active">

At any given time, only one Organization Chart can be in the **In use** state.

When a new chart becomes active, Workflow automatically changes the previous active chart to **End**.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Organization Charts are versioned">

Workflow never overwrites an existing Organization Chart.

Every structural change should be made in a new version, preserving historical organizational information.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Editing does not affect running workflows">

Charts in the **Editing** or **Not in use** state do not affect current Workflow operations.

Only the **In use** Organization Chart is referenced by the system.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Applied Date controls activation">

The Applied Date determines when an Organization Chart becomes effective.

Administrators can prepare future organizational changes before they take effect.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

---

## Version Lifecycle

Every Organization Chart follows the same lifecycle.

```text
Create
   │
   ▼
Editing
   │
   ▼
Configure Departments
Configure Employees
Configure Hierarchy
   │
   ▼
Not in use
   │
   ▼
Set Applied Date
   │
   ▼
Activate
   │
   ▼
In use
   │
   ▼
Superseded by newer version
   │
   ▼
End
```

<Callout type="tip">
  Creating a new version is the recommended approach whenever organizational
  changes are required.
</Callout>

## Department Rules

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Root Department">

Every Organization Chart contains a single root department representing the company.

The root department cannot be deleted.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Department hierarchy">

Departments are organized using parent-child relationships.

A department may contain any number of child departments.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Department movement">

Moving a department automatically moves all of its child departments.

Employee assignments remain attached to the department.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Department deletion">

Workflow supports two deletion methods:

- Delete Department
- Delete Department Branch

Delete Department Branch removes the selected department together with every descendant department.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

---

## Employee Assignment Rules

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Department assignment">

Employees can be assigned directly from the Organization Chart.

Existing employees may be assigned, or new employees may be created during the assignment process.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Multiple department assignments">

An employee may belong to multiple departments.

Each department assignment is managed independently.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Department Role">

Department Roles describe an employee's organizational position.

They are independent from approval routing and system permissions.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>

---

## Approval Authority Rules

Approval Authority identifies employees who officially represent a department.

It is **not** equivalent to a Department Role or an Approver Level.

The following concepts serve different purposes:

| Concept            | Purpose                                                                                                               |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Department Role    | Employee position within the department.                                                                              |
| Approval Authority | Department representative displayed on the Organization Chart and authorized to manage department-level applications. |
| Approver Level     | Approval responsibility within Workflow processes.                                                                    |

---

### Display Rules

Only employees with **Approval Authority** are displayed directly on department nodes.

Example:

```text
Sales Department

Alice
Department Manager

Bob
Assistant Manager

+15 employee(s)

See all
```

The remaining department members are available through the employee list.

This behavior keeps large Organization Charts readable while highlighting department representatives.

## Relationship Between Modules

The Organization Chart provides the organizational foundation used by other Workflow modules.

```text
Organization Chart
        │
        ▼
Departments
        │
        ▼
Employees
        │
        ▼
Approval Authority
        │
        ▼
Workflow Applications
Approval Routing
Attendance
Organization-based Features
```

Changes made to the active Organization Chart are reflected throughout the system.

## Best Practices

<Callout type="tip">

To maintain an accurate organizational structure:

- Duplicate the current Organization Chart before making structural changes.
- Configure an Applied Date for future reorganizations.
- Use Department Roles to describe organizational positions.
- Assign Approval Authority only to department representatives.
- Prefer updating departments instead of deleting them whenever historical information should be preserved.

</Callout>

# Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Can I edit the active Organization Chart directly?">

Although it is technically possible depending on the chart status, the recommended approach is to duplicate the current chart and prepare changes in a new version.

This preserves historical organizational information and minimizes operational risk.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens when a new Organization Chart becomes active?">

Workflow automatically switches the new chart to **In use** and changes the previously active chart to **End**.

All organization-based features immediately reference the newly activated chart.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I prepare organizational changes before they become effective?">

Yes.

Create a new Organization Chart, configure departments and employees, then set an Applied Date.

The new structure will become effective when activated according to the configured schedule.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why are some employees not displayed on department nodes?">

Only employees with **Approval Authority** are displayed directly on department nodes.

Other department members remain available through the department's employee list.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the difference between Department Role, Approval Authority, and Approver Level?">

These concepts have different purposes.

- Department Role describes an employee's position within the department.
- Approval Authority identifies department representatives and grants department-level management capabilities.
- Approver Level defines approval responsibilities within Workflow processes.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can historical Organization Charts be viewed after a new version is activated?">

Yes.

Previous Organization Charts remain available in the **End** state for historical reference and auditing purposes.

</Accordion>

</Accordions>
