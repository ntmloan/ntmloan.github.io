**Workflow** is a multi-tenant platform for approval workflow (BPM) management, letting companies configure approval routes, and manage employees, organization, etc...

The platform has 3 main roles:

- **Super Admin** — manages the whole platform, across multiple companies (tenants)
- **Admin** — manages a single company: employees, organization, approval routes
- **User** — employee: creates applications, approves requests, tracks attendance
