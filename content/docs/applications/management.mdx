title: Management
description: Aggregated oversight view of applications for users with management permission
icon: LayoutDashboard
---

**Management** provides an aggregated, oversight view of applications across a department and its
sub-departments, for users who have management/oversight permission.

Only visible to users with the `isManagement` permission — other users do not see this page in the
navigation.

Includes its own statistics, filters, search, and pagination, separate from **My Applications**.

<Callout type="info">
  The precise boundary between Management and My Applications (exact permission scope and data
  visible to each) still needs confirmation from the development team.
</Callout>
