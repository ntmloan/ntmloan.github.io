List, search, and manage companies (tenants) on the Workflow platform.

Two ways to onboard a new company:

- **Create directly** — a Super Admin fills in the company's information and creates the tenant immediately.
- **Invite by email** — a Super Admin sends an invite link; the prospective company completes its own signup via the link (see Company Signup under Authentication).

<Callout type="info">Only Super Admins can access this feature.</Callout>
