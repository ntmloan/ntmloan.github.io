---
title: Company Administration
description: Admin features for managing a company's people, structure, and approval routes
icon: Settings
---

- **Company Information** — view/edit the company's profile and configuration
- **Representative** — manage delegate/proxy approvers, with a change history
- **Manage Roles** — visual role hierarchy (role chart) defining approval permissions
- **Employees** — employee directory: add, edit, search, filter, export
- **Org Chart** — create and manage the company's organizational structure
- **Application Definitions** — configure the approval route templates applications follow, plus manage Application Categories
- **Attendance** (only for some specific companies) — configure company-wide work shifts and working days
- **Notifications** — connect/disconnect Slack and Microsoft Teams notification integrations
- **Upload History** — log of bulk file uploads (e.g. employee imports)

<Callout type="info">
  **Representative**: the exact business meaning of the "representative" feature still needs
  confirmation from the development team (delegating approval while away?).
</Callout>
